# Beta protocol (weekend 10/10–11)

**Goal:** collect evidence for two PRD success criteria ("dial in a sound in under 10 seconds" and "sounds good at every position") plus stability and quotes.

**Testers:** 2–3 producers, each on their own Mac and their own sessions.

---

## Before
- [ ] Send each tester the signed installer and the quick-start PDF.
- [ ] Ask them to **screen-record the first session** (QuickTime → File → New Screen Recording). The video gives honest first-use timing and possible case-study footage. Get permission to use clips.

## Test 1: time to dial in (first use, no coaching)
1. Open a session of theirs and add BigCity to a vocal or synth.
2. Prompt: *"Get it sounding the way you'd want it on this track. Tell me when you're happy."*
3. **Record:** seconds from first knob touch to "happy," their final City size value, and any hesitation.
- **Target:** under 10 seconds (PRD §4).

## Test 2: blind A/B at four settings
1. Before the session, export 8 bars of one of their tracks at City size 25, 50, 75 and 100, plus bypass. All are loudness-matched by auto gain.
2. Present them in random order, labeled A–E.
3. **Ask:** rate each 1–5 for "sounds good" and "sounds like city-pop sheen," and say which one is bypass.
- **Evidence we want:** every setting rated 3 or higher, and bypass hard to pick at 25.

## Test 3: stability and fit (a normal working session)
- [ ] Any crashes, clicks, or loading problems?
- [ ] CPU or session impact in a dense project (20+ tracks)?
- [ ] **One question:** "What would you tell a friend this does?" This gives a quote and a message test.
